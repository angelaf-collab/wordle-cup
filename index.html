📖 How to play & scoring ▾

The game. One puzzle a day, Monday–Friday. Everyone on the team solves the exact same five-letter word, and you get six tries. After each guess the tiles tell you how close you were:

  • Green — right letter, right spot.
  • Yellow — right letter, wrong spot.
  • Grey — not in the word.

Entering a guess. Type your five letters and press Enter to submit; use Backspace to fix a typo. Every guess must be a real five-letter word — made-up letter combinations won't be accepted, so you never waste a turn on a non-word.

Your timer starts on your first letter and stops the moment you solve it, so think before you type. You get one shot per day — if you leave mid-game and the day rolls over, that attempt is lost.

Your name. Pick your name once and this device remembers you — from then on you always play as yourself, no dropdown to fumble each day.

Scoring — most points wins the Cup. Each day you can earn:

  • Solve points: 6 for solving in 1 guess, 5 for 2, down to 1 for 6 (0 if you don't get it).
  • Speed bonus: +1 to the day's fastest solver.
  • Streak bonus: play all five weekdays in a week to earn a streak point — 1 for your first full week, 2 for the next consecutive full week, then 3, and so on. Miss a day and your streak resets the following Monday.
  • Missed days: −1 point for each weekday you skip (your total never drops below 0). Weekends are off — skipping them never costs you anything.

Ties are broken by the fewest average guesses, then the fastest average time. The leaderboard updates live as teammates play.
